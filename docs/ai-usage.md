# AI usage acknowledgement

The canonical [AI policy and disclosure record](../.github/AI_USAGE.md) applies to issues, commits, PRs and documentation. Disclose the tool, generated/suggested work, the developer’s own design and modifications, and actual verification. Use `None` when no meaningful assistance occurred.

```text
AI Assistance:
Tool: <tool or None>
Used for: <specific suggestion/generated material>
Human contribution: <what I designed, changed, reviewed and tested>
Verification performed: <actual commands/results or pending>
```

Do not claim generated text/code was written manually, attribute team work to yourself, or claim review that has not happened. Add the recommended commit footer from the policy and summarize assistance in each personal contribution, iteration and release record.
