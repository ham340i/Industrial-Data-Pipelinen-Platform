# Starter personas

Hypotheses from the project brief, **pending stakeholder validation**. No interviews or demographic claims are implied.

| Persona | Needs and goals | Candidate pain points | Technical comfort to validate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Process Specialist | Configure repeatable workflows and inspect meaningful errors | Repetitive preparation; opaque transformations | Domain expertise; coding experience varies |
| Data/Systems Integrator | Map approved sources/models and control access | Schema drift, connector/auth complexity | Likely comfortable with schemas/APIs; confirm |
| Engineering Analyst | Trust and trace outputs for analysis | Missing provenance and inconsistent quality | Analytical tools; pipeline engineering varies |
| ML/Data Consumer | Reproducible, versioned, validated inputs | Undetected changes and preparation overhead | Data tooling; manufacturing context varies |
| System Administrator | Manage local configuration, permissions and recovery | Credential exposure and unsupported installs | Operations expertise; ETL familiarity varies |

Validate each through approved interviews/demos: actual workflow, accessibility needs, terminology, success criteria and constraints. Link findings to stories and update assumptions. Do not collect unnecessary personal data.
